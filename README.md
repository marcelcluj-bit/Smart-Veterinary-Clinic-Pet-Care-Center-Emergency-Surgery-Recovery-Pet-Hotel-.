# Smart Veterinary Clinic & ICU Hub: AI Telemetry & Web3 RWA Architecture

## Overview
This repository provides an open-source modular management framework for modern **Veterinary Clinics & Animal Hospitals**. It integrates real-time IoT vital sign monitoring for intensive care units (ICU), automated clinical triage, and Web3 Real World Asset (RWA) tokenomics for high-end medical equipment funding (such as advanced imaging and surgical gear).

## Core Modules

1. **AI Triage & Admission Management:**
   - Automated sorting of incoming animal patients into ICU or general recovery wards.
   - Owner wallet linking and digital health record tracking.

2. **IoT ICU Vital Signs Telemetry:**
   - Continuous remote tracking of heart rate, body temperature, and blood oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$).
   - Real-time automated alerts for veterinary staff upon detection of unstable vitals.

3. **Web3 RWA Equipment Tokenomics:**
   - Fractional tokenized ownership model allowing community investors to fund heavy diagnostic hardware.
   - Automated smart contract revenue sharing distributing monthly equipment lease dividends.
